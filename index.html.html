What I annotate

Four kinds of work, each with its own quality checks.

text

Text

Named entity recognition, sentiment, intent and topic classification, and content moderation labels. I handle edge cases such as sarcasm, mixed sentiment and ambiguous entities, and I note the reasoning behind unclear calls.

image · video

Image and video

Bounding boxes, polygons and segmentation, object tracking across frames, and attribute tagging. I keep edges tight and labels consistent from the first frame to the last.

audio

Audio and transcription

Verbatim and clean transcription, speaker labeling, timestamps, and noise or accent tagging. I follow the style guide for fillers, overlap and unclear speech.

llm

LLM response evaluation

Ranking and comparing model answers, rating helpfulness, accuracy and safety, and writing short justifications. I check facts instead of trusting a confident tone. As a prompt engineer, I also write and test prompts, not just rate the answers.

How I work

The same routine on every project, so results stay consistent.

  1. Read the guidelines twiceI list anything unclear before I label a single item.
  2. Run a small pilot batchI compare it with your gold examples and adjust early.
  3. Label at pace, flag edge casesAmbiguous items get a note, not a guess.
  4. Review my own workI re-check a sample of every batch before delivery.
  5. Report backYou get the data plus a short summary of issues and guideline gaps.
